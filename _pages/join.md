We are a theoretical and computational plasma physics group in the School of Physical and Mathematical Sciences (SPMS) at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. We combine physics-based models, high-performance simulations and machine learning to help make fusion energy a reality.

## What we work on

- **Turbulence and transport** in the plasma core, using gyrokinetic simulations with GYSELA
- **Edge and divertor physics**, using three-dimensional fluid simulations
- **Energetic particles and impurities** and how they interact with turbulence
- **AI and reduced models**: fast surrogate models trained on simulation and experimental data

Our current projects are described on the [Projects](/projects/) page. You are also welcome to propose your own topic.

## PhD positions

We are looking for motivated PhD students in theoretical and computational fusion plasma physics. Positions are fully funded and hosted in SPMS, NTU.

**You should have** a Bachelor's or Master's degree in physics, engineering, applied mathematics or a related field by the time you start.

**Helpful, but not required:**
- Coursework or research in plasma physics
- Experience running simulation codes on high-performance computers
- Experience with modern machine-learning tools

**How to apply:** contact one of the faculty members below about your interests, then apply through NTU. Admission requirements and deadlines are on these pages:
- [SPMS Physics PhD programme](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/spms/about-us/physics/grad/phd)
- [NTU PhD in Physics](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/education/graduate-programme/doctor-of-philosophy-(phd)-in-physics)
- [NTU graduate admission guide](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/admissions/graduate/radmissionguide)
