### Biography 

Ahmad Ibrahim Bin Marzuki is a PhD student in the School of Physical and Mathematical Sciences (SPMS) at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), under the supervision of Prof. Kyungtak Lim. He obtained a BSc (Hons) with a double major in Physics and Mathematical Sciences from NTU in 2026.

### Research Interests 

Ahmad studies resonant magnetic perturbations (RMPs) in tokamaks, with a particular focus on their effects on plasma turbulence, transport, and confinement. He is broadly interested in applying advanced mathematical methods to theoretical and computational problems in plasma physics.
